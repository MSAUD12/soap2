How a bar gets made

Every batch follows the same slow steps, so each bar is a little different and made with care.

Blend

Goat milk, oils and essential oils are mixed with botanicals.

Pour

The batch is poured into molds: bars, ramekins, stars and trees.

Cure

Bars rest and harden, then are cut and trimmed by hand.

Wrap

Each one is boxed or bagged with a purple iris label.
